Priority is separate from state: `incomplete` = missing/partial implementation;
`pending` = scheduled work; `deferred` = outside the delivered baseline;
`decision` = design choice; `verification` = qualification gap.
Owners below are responsible subsystems. [Milestone status](STATUS.md) tracks delivery.
