# VX-SHADOW-01 takeover findings

Review of the unfinished VSM work, 2026-10-12, against UE5.7 and the Vortex
contracts. Every finding has a resolving slice in the
[milestone plan](README.md#slices); the target design is
[virtual-shadow-maps.md](../../lld/virtual-shadow-maps.md).

Scope reviewed:

- Shaders under `Graphics/Direct3D12/Shaders/Vortex/Services/Shadows/Vsm/`
  and `Contracts/Shadows/VsmFrameBindings.hlsli`.
- Vortex hooks: `Vortex/Types/VsmFrameBindings.h`,
  `ViewFrameBindings::virtual_shadow_frame_slot` (a schema field in
  `Vortex/Meta/ShaderContracts.yaml`),
  `ShadowService::HasVsm`, `directional_shadow_vsm_active`,
  `RendererConfig::directional_shadow_policy`.
- The retired `Oxygen.Renderer` VSM runtime (deleted in `58bf8b4a9`), read
  only to explain how the shaders were driven. It is not a reference.
- `design/renderer-shadows/virtual-shadow-map-{architecture,implementation-plan,performance-playbook}.md`.

Paths below are relative to `src/Oxygen/`; shader paths to
`Graphics/Direct3D12/Shaders/Vortex/Services/Shadows/Vsm/`.

## Summary

The page-table/physical-pool vocabulary, the stage breakdown and the
contract-check discipline are sound and survive. Five decisions are not:

1. Page allocation was decided on the CPU from a mid-frame readback of GPU
   page requests (A1). This is the root of the recorded camera-rotation
   failures and the device-hang captures.
2. Clipmap levels were modelled as mips of one map (A2), and mapped-mip
   propagation ran in the wrong direction (A3). Sampling had no coarser
   fallback.
3. Depth-format storage forced scratch copies, per-page dispatches and
   per-page draw lists (A5).
4. All lights were multiplied into one screen mask (A7).
5. Two reversed-Z reductions are inverted (B1, B2).

The C++ runtime does not exist in Vortex. The plan therefore builds a
GPU-driven implementation in Vortex, reusing the shader files whose role
persists.
