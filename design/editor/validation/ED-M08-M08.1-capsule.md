# ED-M08 M08.1 — Capsule and axial primitive increment

Date: 2026-09-16. Status: **implemented and native-test-validated; M08.1 open**.

## Implemented

- Shared native Capsule factory, binary parameters, built-in catalog identity,
  descriptor schema and cooker producer. The default is centred, Z-axis,
  2 m total height and 1 m diameter. Parameters reject invalid counts, non-finite
  dimensions and float32 surface collapse. The sphere limit has no duplicate
  equator or degenerate pole triangles.
- Loaded procedural mesh bounds now derive from generated vertices. Attaching
  a recipe descriptor previously overwrote them with zero bounds. Authored
  Standard/Skinned descriptor bounds retain their existing authority.
- Physics Capsule/Cylinder adapt Jolt's primitive basis to Oxygen Z before
  authored transforms. Capsule total height is `2 * (half_height + radius)`;
  cylindrical half-height zero is the sphere limit. World axes are unchanged.
- The maintained character mesh matches its capsule collider. The bottle's
  cylinder has an explicit local fit to its imported Y-aligned mesh; its mesh
  and node placement are preserved. Existing wheel rotations are preserved.
